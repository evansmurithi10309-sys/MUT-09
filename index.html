MUT House Hunter is your premier digital platform for finding verified, affordable housing near Murang'a University of Technology. We connect students, faculty, and staff directly with landlords in surrounding estates.

Whether you're looking for an economical single room, a self-contained bedsitter, or a spacious apartment, every listing comes complete with photos, transparent pricing, location maps, and verified landlord contact information.
